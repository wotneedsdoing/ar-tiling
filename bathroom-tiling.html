Good to know

Tips before you start

  • Porcelain is the best choice for bathroom floors: harder and almost waterproof.
  • Larger tiles mean fewer grout lines to keep clean.
  • Plan where the shower, taps and shelves go before tiling starts, so cuts land neatly.

Questions

How long does a bathroom take to tile?

It depends on the size and how much preparation is needed. Your quote gives the number of days before any work starts.

Can you tile around a new bathroom suite?

Yes. Tiling usually happens after the plumber has done the first fix and before the final fit of basins and toilets.
