How many tiles do I need?

Measure once, order once. A simple way to work out how many m² of tiles to buy, with a worked example.

4 minute read · by the AR Tiling team · October 2026

1. Measure each surface

Measure in metres. For each wall, multiply the width by the height you'll tile. For a floor, multiply length by width. Split L-shaped rooms into rectangles and add them up.

2. Take away the gaps

Take off doors, windows, the bath panel or anything you won't tile behind. Leave in small gaps like sockets: you'll need those tiles for cuts anyway.

3. Add extra for cuts

Extra to add
LayoutAdd
Straight lay, brick bond, third offset10%
Herringbone, chevron, diagonal15%
Natural stone, handmade tiles, small rooms with lots of cuts15%

Worked example

A shower enclosure with a 1.2 m and a 0.9 m wall, tiled to 2.1 m high: (1.2 + 0.9) × 2.1 = 4.41 m². Add 10%: 4.85 m². Tiles come in boxes of, say, 1.08 m², so order 5 boxes (5.4 m²).

4. Check every box

  • Every box must show the same batch or shade number. Different batches can be a visibly different colour.
  • Order everything at once. Topping up months later may mean a new batch.
  • Keep a few spare tiles for future repairs.

The calculator and tile shop add the extra for you. Or tell us the room sizes and we'll work it out when we quote.
