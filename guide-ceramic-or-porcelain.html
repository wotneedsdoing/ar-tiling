Ceramic or porcelain?

They look alike in the shop but behave very differently. Here's which one to use where.

3 minute read · by the AR Tiling team · October 2026

The difference

Both are made from fired clay. Porcelain uses finer clay fired hotter, so it's denser, harder and soaks up almost no water (under 0.5%). Ceramic is lighter, softer and easier to cut, and comes in the widest choice of colours and glazes.

Our advice

  • Walls: ceramic is perfect and saves money, especially metro and patterned tiles.
  • Floors, showers and wet rooms: porcelain, with a slip rating of R10 or higher for bathrooms and R11 for wet rooms.
  • Wood, marble or stone looks: porcelain gives the look without the sealing and upkeep.
  • Outdoors: only 20 mm outdoor porcelain, laid on a full mortar bed.
