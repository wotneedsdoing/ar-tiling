Good to know

Tips before you start

  • A flat base is everything. Large tiles show every bump underneath.
  • Rectangular tiles look best in a third offset, which hides the slight bow in long tiles.
  • Wood-effect porcelain gives the look of timber with none of the water worries.

Questions

Can you tile over floorboards?

Yes, once they're overboarded or covered with a decoupling membrane, so movement in the timber doesn't crack the tiles.

How soon can I walk on a new floor?

Usually the next day for light use. Heavy furniture and appliances go back once the grout has fully set.
