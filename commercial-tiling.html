Good to know

Tips before you start

  • Send drawings, a tile schedule and your programme for a fixed contract price.
  • Choose slip-rated porcelain (R10 or higher) for public and commercial floors.
  • Epoxy grout is easier to keep hygienic in kitchens and washrooms.
